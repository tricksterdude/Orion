# Audio Subsystem

## Purpose

The Audio subsystem verifies that audio is being delivered correctly through the Home Cinema system.

---

## Current Status

Planned.

---

## Planned Features

- Detect Windows playback device
- HDMI verification
- Bitstream verification
- Audio format verification
- AVR integration

---

## Supported Formats

- Dolby Digital
- Dolby Digital Plus
- Dolby TrueHD
- Dolby Atmos
- DTS
- DTS-HD Master Audio
- DTS:X
- PCM
- FLAC

---

## Long-Term Goal

Verify the complete audio chain from media file to AV Receiver.
